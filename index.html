About
We build homes where light, air and honest material come together - a quieter way of living, ten minutes from the sea.

Surya Grand Developers is a Visakhapatnam studio with three ongoing projects across Madhurawada, Yendada and Anandapuram - 2 & 3 BHK flats and VUDA-approved plots.

Every enquiry is answered by our AI receptionist in under 30 seconds, in Telugu or English, day or night. Prices, photos, videos, floor plans, site visits - just ask.
